channel.create {_data.agent?, _data.cwd, _data.text?, _data.model?, _data.mode?, _data.effort?, _data.preset?, _data.role?, _data.nick?, _data.intro?, _data.persona?, _data.avatar?, data.name?, data.parent_id?, guild_id?} -> Channel  start a session, a child of yours; _data.cwd=. your folder. Another agent to work with in this chat: invite it instead (POST internal/channels/{id}/members)
  data.parent_id: the session it hangs under on the page (its launcher). Not given: the session your ir runs in, so one you start is your child: listed under you, archived with you. To start one of its own: channel.update data.parent_id:=null after.
  _data.cwd: . is your own folder and a relative path goes from it; ~ and ~/x the home; host:path here; app:path at that app's computer (GET internal/apps), where it then runs; an absolute path as it is.
  guild_id: a folder group's id (guild.list), self yours; not given: its folder's group, yours when yours holds its folder.
  _data.agent: not given: a channel with no agent of its own, listed in _data.cwd; agents join it (POST internal/channels/{id}/members) and people speak in it. Given: claude, codex, pi, dsh or agy (GET internal/agents: those that can start here, their modes); _data.mode: one of them; model, effort, preset: the agent's own (SessionState.models, efforts; GET internal/sessions/{id}/presets).
  _data.role: start it as a kept role (GET internal/roles): its agent, model (dsh: preset) and card; effort and mode are this start's (_data.effort, _data.mode) or the agent's own. _data.nick, _data.intro, _data.persona, _data.avatar, _data.background: its card from the start (ir api help profile).
  _data.text: its first message, from you: it reads [ir:<your session>] before it. ir prints the id, with text once the agent took it. Started from a session, it joins that session's channel: the text is then said to it there, its answer said there.
  --wait: holds your turn till its first turn ends, then prints its reply: for a session that does a job apart from your channel and reports to you. Not for an agent in your channel (one you invited or started there): its words are said in the channel and its answer to you calls you, so waiting only holds your turn and gets you words everyone has already seen.

# scripts
GET internal/channels/{id}/scripts - -> Script[]  the scripts the channel's IR (Iridium) runs: name, intro, run, on, enabled, who made and last changed it, its last run  view=name,intro,on,enabled,run
POST internal/channels/{id}/scripts {name, run, on, intro?, avatar?, enabled?} -> Script  add a script: a command line run at the host in the channel's folder when one of on fires; any member  [more]
  on: a list, each one of {"message":{"from":[…],"words":[…]}} (senders as recv names them; words: any of them; none: anything), {"command":"name"} (a person's /name: goes to the script, not the agents), {"cron":"m h dom mon dow"}, {"event":["join","leave","rename","recv","people"]}.
  Given the message as JSON on stdin and as IR_TEXT, IR_FROM, IR_ARGS, IR_TRIGGER. What it prints (exit 0) is said under its name; @nick in it wakes that agent; ir in it speaks as the script. 60 s at most. E.g. on:='[{"message":{"from":["*"],"words":["<word>"]}}]' run='echo "@<nick> $IR_TEXT"'.
PATCH internal/channels/{id}/scripts/{name} {name?, intro?, avatar?, run?, on?, enabled?} -> Script  change a script; enabled:=false keeps it and stops it running
DELETE internal/channels/{id}/scripts/{name} - -> {ok}  take a script away, its runs with it
GET internal/channels/{id}/scripts/{name}/runs - -> ScriptRun[]  its latest runs, newest first: trigger, code, what it printed (out), its errors (err), said: the message it became  view=at,trigger,code,said,err
POST internal/channels/{id}/scripts/{name}/run {text?} -> ScriptRun  run it now, given text as the message; what it does is said as ever
